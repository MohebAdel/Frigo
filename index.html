About Frigo Global Group

Global Trading Backbone for International Food Security

The Cold Chain Advantage

Zero Tolerance for Temperature Fluctuations

How Frigo Global Group guarantees food integrity across 10,000 nautical miles.

01

Origin Blast Freezing

Core temperature brought to -38°C in industrial spiral or air-blast freezing tunnels within hours of harvest to preserve cellular structure and moisture.

02

IoT Reefer Containerization

State of the art 40ft High Cube refrigerated containers equipped with satellite GPS dataloggers tracking temperature, humidity, and airflow 24/7.

03

Dedicated Maritime Corridors

Contracted slots with premier ocean carriers (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM) ensuring expedited transit times and direct discharge routes.

04

Port Clearance & Delivery

Rapid veterinary clearance at major destination ports with seamless plug-in cold yards and direct handover to wholesale buyers.
